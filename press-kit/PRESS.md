### Built with

- **Narration:** Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (voice `af_heart`), run offline by `ios/tools/build_voice_pack_kokoro.py`. An older pack used Piper.
- **Content:** math questions generated by code; reading, science and social studies questions written and then checked by Anthropic's Claude, called through the Claude Code CLI (`claude --print`) in `ios/tools/generate_content.py` and `judge_content.py`.
- **Purchases:** Apple StoreKit 2 on iOS, Google Play Billing on Android.
- **Fonts:** Fredoka, Bubblegum Sans and Outfit from Google Fonts.

The app shells, learning engine, game UI, content pipeline and tests are Matt's code. The models and libraries above are upstream projects and are credited as such.

## Description, 150 words

Brainforest is a learning game for kids in kindergarten through 4th grade, on iPhone, iPad and Android. A child picks a theme, like dinosaurs, space or mermaids, and answers questions to climb a treehouse, collecting stickers and a named treasure at the end of each quest.

There are 2,039 activities covering math, phonics, reading, spelling, science, social studies and feelings, plus 726 "Did you know?" facts. Every question and answer choice is spoken by a recorded narrator, so kids who can't read yet can play on their own. Math uses real pictures: analog clocks, coins, fraction pies. The engine tracks mastery per skill, moves up only when a child is ready, and brings missed questions back later.

Everything runs on the device. No internet, no ads, no accounts, and no data collected. A parent dashboard shows progress per child. It's free for 60 days, then a one-time $1.99 unlock.

## Open questions for Matt (not for publishing)

- **Fonts and "no network connections":** `ios/web/style.css:2` and the Android copy `@import` Google Fonts when the device is online, and `AndroidManifest.xml` declares INTERNET for that reason. `PRIVACY.md` says the app "makes no network connections." The press copy here avoids that exact phrase and says the app doesn't need the internet. Worth deciding whether to bundle the fonts or adjust the policy wording.
- **Contact email:** README uses info@nicedreamzwholesale.com, PRIVACY.md uses a personal address. Press copy uses the info@ address.
- **Gift codes for reviewers:** the email offers one. Confirm you want to hand those out.

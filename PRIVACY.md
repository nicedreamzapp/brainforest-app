# Privacy Policy — Brainforest (Android & iOS)

**Effective date: July 7, 2026**

Brainforest is built by Nice Dreamz LLC. This policy covers the Brainforest mobile app on Android and iOS.

The public, always-current copy of this policy lives at:
**https://hq.nicedreamzwholesale.com/brainforest-privacy.html**

## The short version

**Brainforest collects no data. None.** Everything your child does in the app stays on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. Brainforest has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and makes no network connections. The app works completely offline.

## Where your child's progress lives

All progress — stickers, treasures, skill mastery, and practice history — is stored only on your device. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. We never see it, and neither does anyone else.

## Children's privacy (COPPA)

Brainforest is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act the simplest way possible: it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

## Third parties

There are none. No third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics providers are included in the app.

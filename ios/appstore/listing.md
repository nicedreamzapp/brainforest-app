## Description
Built by a dad for his two daughters — and now for your family too.

Brainforest is a complete K–4th grade learning adventure that works 100% offline. No ads. No subscriptions. No accounts. No data collection — ever. Your child climbs a magical treehouse by answering questions, earning stickers and treasures along the way.

WHAT MAKES BRAINFOREST DIFFERENT
• Works ANYWHERE — airplanes, road trips, grandma's house. No internet needed, ever.
• No subscription. No ads. No tracking. The privacy label is empty because we collect nothing.
• A warm, friendly narrator reads every question and answer choice out loud — perfect for pre-readers.
• Real teaching visuals: analog clocks for telling time, actual coins for counting money, fraction pies, shapes, and countable objects for every math problem.
• Adapts to your child: starts gentle, grows only when mastery is proven, and brings missed questions back for another try.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 2,000+ activities across Kindergarten through 4th grade
• Math: counting, adding, subtracting, multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, time, money, geometry, word problems
• Reading: phonics, sight words, spelling, vocabulary, grammar, reading comprehension
• Science, social studies, and feelings (SEL)
• 7 fun themes: unicorns, mermaids, dinosaurs, space, cats, horses, puppies
• Treehouse quests, a forever sticker book, named treasures, and daily streaks
• 700+ "Did you know?" facts that stretch one grade ahead
• Letter tracing for little hands
• Parent dashboard: skill mastery, accuracy, and session history — stored only on your device

MULTIPLE KIDS, ONE APP
Add each of your kids with their own grade. Everyone gets their own progress, stickers, and treasures.
